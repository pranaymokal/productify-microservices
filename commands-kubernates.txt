2. Accessing Swagger in Kubernetes (Minikube or AKS)
In Kubernetes, backend services run on internal cluster IPs (ClusterIP). To open Swagger UI in your browser on your machine, use kubectl port-forward:

powershell
# For Product Service (Port 8086)
kubectl port-forward svc/product-service 8086:8086
# For User Service (Port 8085)
kubectl port-forward svc/user-service 8085:8085
# For Order Service (Port 8087)
kubectl port-forward svc/order-service 8087:8087
# For Cart Service (Port 8084)
kubectl port-forward svc/cart-service 8084:8084
# For Auth Service (Port 8083)
kubectl port-forward svc/auth-service 8083:8083
# For Analytics Service (Port 8088)
kubectl port-forward svc/analytics-service 8088:8088
